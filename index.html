ABOUT THE MUSEUM

Preserving the History of Binalonan

Museo de Binalonan is dedicated to preserving, documenting, and showcasing the rich cultural heritage of the municipality through historical artifacts, archival materials, photographs, and community stories.

The museum serves as a learning space where residents, students, and visitors can discover the people, traditions, and events that shaped Binalonan through generations.
